# Website Launch Checklist for Small Businesses

Launching a business website is more than publishing a few pages. A reliable launch should cover user experience, mobile usability, technical SEO, analytics, security, accessibility and conversion paths.

This practical checklist is maintained by **Vylino**, a website design, development and SEO agency serving businesses in India and international markets.

## 1. Business and conversion essentials

- Confirm the primary business goal for the website: enquiries, bookings, sales, calls or WhatsApp conversations.
- Make the main call to action visible on important pages.
- Check that phone numbers, email addresses, forms and messaging links work on desktop and mobile.
- Keep service descriptions specific enough for visitors to understand what is offered and who it is for.
- Add trust signals such as genuine testimonials, portfolio work, policies and business information where appropriate.

## 2. Mobile and user experience

- Test important pages on common mobile screen sizes.
- Check navigation, buttons, forms and tap targets.
- Avoid oversized pop-ups that block the main content.
- Keep headings, body copy and spacing readable without zooming.
- Verify that important information appears in a sensible order on smaller screens.

## 3. Technical SEO

- Use one clear primary heading on each important page.
- Write a unique SEO title and meta description for pages that should appear in search.
- Use descriptive URLs and avoid unnecessary parameters for core landing pages.
- Add canonical URLs where appropriate.
- Make sure search engines can access indexable pages through internal links.
- Generate and submit an XML sitemap.
- Review robots.txt so important pages are not accidentally blocked.
- Redirect replaced URLs instead of leaving avoidable 404 errors.

## 4. Content and on-page SEO

- Match each important page to a distinct search intent instead of forcing every keyword onto one page.
- Use natural primary and supporting terms in headings and body copy.
- Add useful internal links between related services, industries, locations and resources.
- Use descriptive image alt text where an image conveys meaningful information.
- Avoid duplicating near-identical location or service pages without genuinely useful local or topic-specific content.
- Include FAQs only when they answer real customer questions.

## 5. Performance

- Compress and resize large images before upload.
- Prefer modern image formats when supported.
- Limit unnecessary scripts, fonts, animations and third-party widgets.
- Test page speed on both mobile and desktop.
- Use caching and a CDN when they fit the hosting setup.
- Check Core Web Vitals and investigate major layout shifts or slow loading elements.

## 6. Analytics and search visibility

- Install an analytics platform and confirm page views are being recorded.
- Connect the website to Google Search Console.
- Submit the sitemap and monitor indexing.
- Track meaningful conversions such as form submissions, calls, purchases or WhatsApp clicks.
- Keep campaign tracking consistent when using paid advertising or social campaigns.

## 7. Security and maintenance

- Enable HTTPS and confirm there are no mixed-content warnings.
- Use strong administrator credentials and appropriate access permissions.
- Keep the CMS, plugins, themes and server software updated.
- Maintain automated backups and test the restore process.
- Remove unused plugins, themes and accounts.
- Protect forms from obvious spam without making them difficult for real users.

## 8. Final pre-launch checks

- Proofread key pages.
- Test forms and confirmation messages.
- Check links, buttons and navigation.
- Test the 404 page.
- Verify favicon, social sharing image and basic brand assets.
- Confirm legal and policy pages required for the business model.
- Recheck the site after DNS or hosting changes.
